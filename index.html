Foam Impact Design Calculator

This tool encodes the iterative impact design method from the example into a simple interface. Inputs use your chosen maximum allowable impact force Fmax (on the payload), and foam options are based on Polyethylene and Cross-linked Polyethylene sheets (Foam Factory data). The yellow columns on the right give the optimum foam thickness and relative density.
Use either h or v (if both are given, v is used). Es in MPa. Fmax in N.
