Foam Impact Design Calculator

Iterative impact design from the example implemented as a web tool. You can now specify the actual foam density ρ* (kg/m³) for each case to see how the required thickness changes for different PE / XLPE grades. The yellow columns on the right show the optimum foam thickness and ρ*/ρs.
Use either h or v (if both are given, v is used). Fmax is the allowable impact force on the payload (N). Es is the solid polymer modulus basis (MPa). ρ* is the chosen foam density (kg/m³).
